# Privacy Policy

_Placeholder text — must be reviewed by a lawyer for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before launch._

## What we collect
- Your name, mobile number and, if you give them, email, city, education and photo.
- What you do in courses: lessons completed, exam answers and results, attendance, questions you ask.
- Technical data needed to keep your account safe: device type and IP address at login.

## Why we collect it
- To log you in with a one-time password sent to your mobile.
- To give your teacher what they need to teach you: your name, mobile number, progress and results.
- To send you messages about your classes. WhatsApp messages are sent only if you agreed to them.

## Who can see it
- Your teacher sees your details for their courses only.
- Other students never see your mobile number.
- The platform admin sees teacher accounts and can help with account problems.
- We do not sell your data.

## Your choices
- You can change your mobile number or delete your account from Profile → Account at any time.
- When you delete your account we remove your name, mobile, email and photo. Exam and attendance records stay with your teacher as "Deleted user".

## Contact
Questions about your data: see the support contact shown in the app.
