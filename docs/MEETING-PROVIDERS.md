# Live class providers: Google Meet and Zoom

Plans, prices, recording and attendance for running live classes, with links to the official
documentation. Prices are from the official India pricing pages as of **19 Nov 2026**; check the
links before buying, because plans and feature lists change.

## 1. How licences work

- A **licence** is an account that hosts meetings. Students join for free and need no licence.
- Plans are bought on the **VengurlaTech company account**. Teachers do not get the password;
  they join with their own Gmail and are made co-host.
- **One licence runs one class at a time.** With different class times, one licence can serve
  10–11 teachers (1-hour classes, 15-minute gap, 7 am – 9 pm).
- **Licences needed = the most classes running at the same time**, not the number of teachers.
  Two teachers teaching at the same time need two licences.

## 2. Google Meet (Google Workspace)

### Plans (₹ per user per month)

| Plan | Intro offer* | Regular | Participants | Max length | Recording | Attendance report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free Gmail | – | ₹0 | 100 | **60 min** (3+ people) | No | No |
| **Base** | ₹49.50 | ₹99 | 100 | 24 h | No | No |
| Starter | ₹270 | ₹270 | 100 | 24 h | No | No |
| **Standard** | ₹864 | ₹1,080 | 150 | 24 h | Yes | No |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | – | 1,000 | 24 h | Yes | Yes |

\* New customers only, first 20 users, first 12 months.

- Billed monthly with a 1-year commitment (a flexible monthly plan costs more). GST is extra.
- Base allows at most **20 users**; Starter and Standard up to 300.
- 14-day free trial.
- Official pricing: <https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_in/pricing>

### Recording in Google Meet

- **Plans:** Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise, Education Plus. **Not** Base, Starter
  or free Gmail.
- **Who can record:** the meeting host, or a user in the **same organisation** as the host
  (`@vengurlatech.com`). A teacher who joins with a personal Gmail is outside the organisation
  and likely **cannot** start a recording. Confirm this during the trial.
- **Admin setting:** recording must be turned on in the Admin console.
- **Where it is saved:** the organiser's Google Drive, in the **Meet Recordings** folder. The link
  is emailed to the organiser and to the person who started the recording.
- **Devices:** computer or Android. A recording stops automatically after **8 hours**.
- Docs: [Record a video meeting](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681?hl=en) ·
  [Manage Meet settings (for admins)](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7304109?hl=en)

### Attendance in Google Meet

- **Plans:** Business Plus, Enterprise, Education Plus. **Not** Base or Standard. Business Plus is
  not listed on the India pricing page, so in practice this means Enterprise.
- **What you get:** after the meeting, the organiser receives a **Google Sheets** report with each
  participant's name, email, join and leave times, and total time.
- **How to turn it on:** Host controls in the meeting, or the conference settings of the
  Calendar event.
- Docs: [Track attendance & view Live stream report](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10090454?hl=en) ·
  [Premium Meet features by edition](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10459644?hl=en)

## 3. Zoom

### Plans (₹ per user, excluding GST)

| Plan | Per month (shown for comparison) | Per year (paid upfront) | Participants | Max length | Classes at once per licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic (free) | ₹0 | ₹0 | 100 | **40 min** | 1 |
| **Pro** | ₹1,239.25 | ₹14,871 | 100 | 30 h | 1 |
| Business | ₹1,704.25 | ₹20,451 | 300 | 30 h | 2 |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | – | 1,000 | 30 h | – |

- **Billed annually as one upfront charge.** A monthly plan exists at a higher price.
- Pro allows 1–99 licences per account; Business 1–250.
- "Classes at once per licence" comes from Zoom Community answers, not an official page.
  Confirm with Zoom Sales.
- Official pricing: <https://zoom.us/pricing>

### Recording in Zoom

| Type | Plans | Who can record | Where it is saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Computer (local) | Free and paid | Host and co-host; the host can allow a participant | The computer (`Documents/Zoom`) |
| Cloud | Paid (Pro and above) | **Host and co-host only** | Zoom cloud (Pro: 10 GB per licence); the host gets an email when it is ready |

- The mobile app records to the cloud only.
- Docs: [Getting started with computer and cloud recording](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059856) ·
  [Starting a computer recording](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0076922) ·
  [Starting a cloud recording on iOS and Android](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0066238) ·
  [All recording articles](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/category?id=kb_category&kb_category=55597a9a8720391089a37408dabb353e)

### Attendance in Zoom

- **Plans:** Pro and above; the meeting must be hosted by a paid account.
- **What you get:** web portal → Reports → Usage → the meeting → participants, with each
  participant's join time, leave time and duration. Generate it about **30 minutes** after the
  meeting ends.
- **Who can see it:** the account owner, or an admin with report permissions.
- Docs: [Getting started with Zoom reporting](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0060623) ·
  [Using Attendee Log](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0058383) ·
  [Generating Active Host reports](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059073) ·
  [Time limits for Zoom Meetings](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0067966)
